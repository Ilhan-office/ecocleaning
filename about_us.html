Our story

A fresh approach to everyday cleaning

EcoCleaning was established in 2015 to provide safe, eco-friendly alternatives to chemical-heavy cleaning services.

Our mission is “A spotless home, without harming the planet.” We use only toxin-free, plant-based cleaning solutions.
